📋 1. Executive Summary & Core Rules

When a Medical Representative (MedRep) submits an institution proposal or profiles a physician using an unverified facility that Sales Force Effectiveness (SFE) subsequently rejects, the HCP Profiling system enforces a 5-point production guarantee:

  • Immediate Multi-DocType Reflection: The rejection cause note is atomically persisted across HCP, HCP Account, and HCP Profile Submission.
  • Lockscreen Heads-Up Notification: A high-priority public notification alerts the MedRep on their lockscreen or homescreen—even when logged out of the app.
  • Hard Profiling Block: The HCP Profiling Wizard actively detects the rejected institution and blocks submission with a red modal/snackbar, preventing corrupted facilities from entering the ERPNext masterlist.
  • SFE Remediation & Remapping Hub: SFE Specialists can remap the rejected institution to an approved masterlist facility in 1-click, updating all affiliated records and clearing the MedRep's block.
  • Label Standardization: Uniform terminology across all screens: Doctor Listing → HCP and Doctor Account → HCP Account.

🔔 4. Lockscreen & Logged-Out Notification Engine

To guarantee Medical Representatives never miss an institution rejection while in the field, notifications leverage native platform channels:

  • Public Lockscreen Visibility: Configured with NotificationVisibility.public and Importance.max so heads-up cards display immediately on Android/iOS lockscreens without requiring device unlock.
  • Logged-Out Detection: Active MedRep email is stored in SharedPreferences under last_active_medrep_user. During background sync or app startup before login, NotificationService.checkAndNotifyPendingRejections() delivers the alert.
🚨 Sample Lockscreen Notification Payload:
Title: 🚨 Institution Proposal Rejected
Body: Proposed workplace 'ST. JUDE COMMUNITY CLINIC' was rejected by SFE.
Reason: Facility merged with Metropolitan Medical Center; use official hospital ID.
